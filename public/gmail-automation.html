Personal Gmail Notification Automation

This is a personal automation that uses read-only Gmail access to detect new messages selected by the account owner and prepare AI-generated notifications or summaries for that owner. It is not available for public sign-up.

To provide this feature, message content may be processed by the AI service selected for a run, and notifications may be sent through the owner's selected messaging service.
