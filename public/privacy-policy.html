Gmail automation

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

This policy covers a personal Gmail notification automation operated by Amir Off for the account owner's use. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

Google data accessed and purpose

With the account owner's authorization, the automation uses read-only Gmail access. It is configured to process new messages matching a Gmail label selected by the account owner. It may read message sender, subject, date, and body to prepare a notification or summary. Gmail change notifications are used to detect new mail; message content is fetched from Gmail when the automation processes it.

How data is used and shared

The automation runs on self-hosted infrastructure. Message content needed for analysis or a summary is sent to the AI service selected for that run. A resulting notification or summary may be sent to the account owner's selected messaging service. Google, the AI service, and the messaging service process data under their own terms and privacy practices.

Google user data is used only to provide the requested notification and summarization. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used for unrelated purposes. It is not shared with other people except as needed by the services described above to operate this feature.

Storage and retention

Message content and summaries may remain in local agent session history or operational logs. Summaries may remain in the account owner's messaging history, and service providers may retain data under their own settings and policies. The account owner can stop access by revoking the automation's Google Account permission and can delete locally retained session records or messages from the relevant services.

Security and changes

The automation is intended for personal use on infrastructure controlled by its operator. No internet-connected system can be guaranteed completely secure. This policy will be updated if the automation's data use materially changes.
